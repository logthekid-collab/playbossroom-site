Privacy policy

Last updated November 8, 2026

Daily Dungeon is a habit game made by Boss Room Studios LLC. We collect as little as we can, and we never sell your data.

Who we are

  • Boss Room Studios LLC, a Delaware limited liability company
  • Contact: logan@playbossroom.com

What the game saves

  • Your hero, habits (the names, schedules, targets and notes you add), progress, purchases and settings
  • It saves on your device and on our servers (Google Firebase), so it can follow you to a new phone
  • Nothing you type is used for ads or shared with anyone

Health data

  • Only if you turn on Apple Health or Health Connect in Settings
  • The game reads today's steps, workout minutes and sleep hours on your device to check off matching habits
  • It never saves, sells or shares your health data, and never uses it for ads
  • You can turn it off any time in Settings or in your phone's Health settings

Friends, clans and the arena

  • Clan chat messages can be seen by everyone in your clan. The game hides slurs automatically
  • You can report or block any player from their profile or a chat message. Reports are only seen by us
  • Cheers, nudges and co-op bosses show your username to that friend
  • Friends find you by the username you pick. Friend requests show the other player your username and hero card
  • If you add friends, join a clan, or use leaderboards or the arena, other players can see your hero card
  • Your hero card is your username, hero name, look, class and level, your top streak, habits finished this week, best dungeon floor, bosses beaten and arena trophies
  • Never your habit names or anything else you type, apart from your hero name and clan names
  • Start over removes your hero card. Leaving a clan removes you from it

Anonymous usage stats

  • Simple events, like which screens you open, fights you start and buttons you tap in the shop
  • No name, no email, no habit names, nothing you type, and no advertising ID
  • They help us find what is confusing and fix it
  • You can turn this off in Settings and see exactly what is recorded

Ads

  • The App Store and Google Play versions have optional rewarded ads
  • An ad only plays when you tap a button that shows the reward first
  • Never before or during a fight, on habit screens, or when the app opens or closes

Services we use

  • Google Firebase stores your account, save, hero card, friends, clan and messages
  • Apple or Google handle purchases

Purchases

  • Purchases go through Apple or Google
  • We never see your card or payment details. We only get a confirmation so we can unlock what you bought

Reminders

  • If you turn on the daily reminder, it is scheduled on your device
  • You can turn it off in Settings

Children

  • Daily Dungeon is for ages 13 and up
  • The app versions use your phone's age signal (Apple's Declared Age Range or Google Play Age Signals)
  • If it says you are under 13, no account is made and nothing is saved

Your choices

  • Turn off anonymous usage stats, Health and reminders in Settings
  • Start over in Settings erases your game data from this device and your account
  • Delete account in Settings removes your hero, username, friends, clan membership, messages and saved progress for good
  • Purchases can be restored with Restore purchases
  • To ask about your data, email logan@playbossroom.com

Changes

  • If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the date at the top
