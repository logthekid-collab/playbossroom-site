Daily Dungeon

A pixel RPG where your real habits power your hero.

Check off a real habit and your hero gets XP, gold and cards. Then use them on bosses named after bad habits, like the Snooze Slime and the Burnout Dragon.

Coming soon to iPhone

Your habits

  • Pick habits from 8 areas, or add your own
  • Any schedule: certain days, a few times a week, or every few days
  • Count things like cups of water or pages read

Your hero

  • 6 classes: Warrior, Mage, Rogue, Cleric, Tamer and Ninja
  • Over 100 cards, from Common to Godlike
  • 64 story bosses across 8 chapters, plus a daily Dungeon run

Your friends

  • Add friends by username and cheer their streaks
  • Join a clan and beat a weekly clan boss together
  • Challenge a friend to a habit duel
