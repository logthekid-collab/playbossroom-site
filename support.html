Support

Daily Dungeon help

Stuck, found a bug, or have an idea? Email logan@playbossroom.com. In the game you can also open Settings (the gear at the top) and tap Send feedback.

Your progress

  • Your hero saves to your account on its own, so you can close the app any time
  • Need a break? Settings, Vacation or sick mode pauses your dailies with no HP loss
  • Start over: Settings, Account, Start over. This erases your hero

Delete your account

  • In the game: Settings, Account, Delete account, then type DELETE
  • This removes your hero, username, friends, clan membership, messages and saved progress for good
  • Can't open the app? Email logan@playbossroom.com with your username and we will delete it for you

Purchases

  • Restore purchases: Settings, Account, Restore purchases
  • Cancel the Dungeon Pass: iPhone Settings, your name, Subscriptions. You keep it until the end of the time you paid for
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com

Apple Health and reminders

  • Turn Apple Health on or off in Settings, Health. The game only reads steps and workouts, and never writes to Health
  • Reminders and chest alerts are in Settings, Reminders

Safety

  • Report or block a player: tap them, then tap Report or Block
  • See who you blocked: Settings, Privacy, Blocked players
